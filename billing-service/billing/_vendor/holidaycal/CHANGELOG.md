# holidaycal changelog

## 1.3.0 (2026-08-03)
- `is_weekend`.
- Floating holidays are defined in one table, `_FLOATING`; `_nth_weekday` takes n = -1 for the last weekday of the
  month, replacing `_last_weekday`.

## 1.2.0 (2025-09-14)
- `next_business_day` and `previous_business_day`.

## 1.1.0 (2023-02-02)
- Juneteenth National Independence Day (from 2021 on).

## 1.0.1 (2022-01-10)
- New Year's Day on a Saturday is observed on the Friday before, in the previous year.

## 1.0.0 (2021-11-30)
- First release: `holidays`, `is_holiday`, `is_business_day`.
